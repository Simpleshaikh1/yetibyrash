Decisions shared by all three

Everyday vs Premium

  • One shop with a two-way switch, not two separate shops. "Everyday" is affordable, "Premium" is the high end.
  • Premium items get a dark label and a slightly richer product page (gift box option, care card, larger gallery).
  • Price filters in ₦ bands: under ₦10k, ₦10k–30k, ₦30k–80k, ₦80k+.

Mixed photo quality

  • Every product image sits on a soft tinted square, so phone shots and studio shots look consistent side by side.
  • The admin upload screen crops to a square, shows a preview and warns when a photo is too small or dark.
  • Studio photos lead on Premium products; phone shots can appear as "real life" extra images.

Paying and tracking

  • Checkout ends in Paystack (card, bank transfer, USSD). The order exists only after Paystack confirms the payment.
  • Tracker beads: Paid → Packed → Shipped → Out for delivery → Delivered, with the city it's in.
  • Track by order number + phone number, no login needed. Updates by email and WhatsApp/SMS.

Phone-first patterns

  • Bottom navigation bar, sticky "Add to bag" button, filters that slide up from the bottom.
  • Nigerian address form: state, then LGA, then street, plus a landmark field.
  • Light pages that load fast on mobile data, with lazily loaded images.

Logo: the name in the mockups is set in each direction's display font as a placeholder. When you send the logo, the chosen direction's colours can be adjusted to match it.
